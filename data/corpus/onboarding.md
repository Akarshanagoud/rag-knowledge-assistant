# Customer Onboarding

## Timeline
Standard onboarding takes two weeks from contract signature. Enterprise onboarding
with SSO configuration and data migration typically takes four to six weeks.

## Required information
Before kickoff the customer provides an administrative contact, the identity provider
metadata if SSO is required, and a list of initial workspaces. Data migration requires
a sample export for schema validation.

## Training
Two live training sessions are included: an administrator session and an end user
session. Recordings are made available in the help centre. Additional sessions are
billed as professional services.
