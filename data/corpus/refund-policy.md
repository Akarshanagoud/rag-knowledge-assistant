# Refund and Cancellation Policy

## Standard refund window
Customers may request a full refund within 30 days of the original purchase date.
Refunds requested after 30 days are handled at the discretion of the support team
and are normally issued as account credit rather than a return to the original
payment method.

## Processing time
Approved refunds are processed within 5 to 7 business days. The funds may take a
further 3 to 5 business days to appear on the customer's statement depending on
their bank. Customers who have not seen the refund after 12 business days should
contact support with their order number.

## Annual subscriptions
Annual subscriptions may be cancelled at any time. Cancellation stops the next
renewal but does not trigger an automatic refund of the current term. A prorated
refund of the unused portion is available within the first 60 days of the annual
term.

## Non-refundable items
Setup fees, professional services engagements and usage-based overage charges are
non-refundable. Discounted promotional purchases are refundable only as account
credit.
