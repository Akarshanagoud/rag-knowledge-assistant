# Data Retention Standard

## Retention periods
Application logs are retained for 90 days. Audit records are retained for 7 years
to satisfy financial reporting obligations. Customer content is retained for the
lifetime of the account plus 30 days after deletion, after which it is purged from
primary storage.

## Backups
Encrypted backups are kept for 35 days on a rolling basis. Deleted customer content
may persist in backups until the backup expires. Backups are never used to restore
individual deleted records at customer request.

## Deletion requests
A verified deletion request is honoured within 30 days. Records subject to a legal
hold are exempt until the hold is released. The deletion confirmation includes the
date and the categories of data removed.
