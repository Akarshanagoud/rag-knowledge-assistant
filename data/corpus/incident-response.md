# Incident Response Procedure

## Severity levels
A Sev1 incident is a total loss of service or a confirmed data breach. A Sev2 is a
major degradation affecting many customers. A Sev3 is a partial degradation with a
workaround available. Severity is assigned by the incident commander and may be
revised.

## Response targets
Sev1 incidents are acknowledged within 15 minutes at any hour. Sev2 incidents are
acknowledged within 1 hour during business hours. Status page updates are published
every 30 minutes during an active Sev1.

## Postmortems
Every Sev1 and Sev2 requires a written postmortem within five business days. The
postmortem is blameless and must include a timeline, contributing factors and
tracked remediation items with owners.
